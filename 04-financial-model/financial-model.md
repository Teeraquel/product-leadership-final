> **The case in one paragraph:** Business Case: Personalized Daily Check-Ins The ask Fund opt-in personalized daily check-ins (Rock #6) for Q4 2026 launch, with a binding kill criterion at 8 weeks and end of Q1 2027. The bet protects about $529K ARR by end of Q3 2027 and tests whether our historical-data differentiator is real. Open question: build and staffing cost. The return below needs to clear it. The bet • Outcome: Current users aged 25–30 who opt in get daily check-ins built on their own sleep history. They sleep better (KR1) and stay longer (KR2). • Who it serves: 336,000 users, the 8% of our 4.2M base in the target group. • Mechanism: Personalized check-ins lead to better-reported sleep. Better sleep cuts quarterly churn among opted-in users from 10% to 7%. Each retained user protects about $70/year in revenue. • Strategic test: The opt-in rate tells us whether users will let us use their history, which is the basis of how we win. Expected return The bet protects about $529K ARR by end of Q3 2027 and delivers 37.5% of the KR2 churn goal.
