Module 1
Product strategy
Where you play and how you win

Winning aspiration: Young professionals ages 25-30 (current highest usage segment) continue to use Fable after sleep improves, and also choose to use the app for a personalized enhanced experience that provides them additional self-care support beyond sleep.
Where to play: United States; users ages 25-30 professionals
How to win: Historical data for Fable's existing customers in our target group which provides insights about sleep patterns and other habits and activities that they currently enjoy, allowing for personalization built on what we’ve learned about our existing users

The hard no

We will not sell data or share it with employers, insurers or advertisers or use it for anything outside the product, unless a user directs us to

Objective and key results

- Objective: Become the app young professionals rely on for sleep, and increasingly for self-care beyond it
- KR1: Increase 90-day retention among target users who report improved sleep from 30% to 60% by end of Q3 2027.
- KR2: Reduce quarterly churn in the target group from 10% to 8% by end of Q3 2027. -
-KR3: Self-care users whose sleep improved retain better than a matched holdout group by at least 10 points by the end of Q2

Module 2
Outcome roadmap
Now, next, and later

Now 0-3 Months: Keep users and improve sleep: fix notification timing (#4), launch a non-sleep content library (#12), and personalized check-ins for opted-in users (#6)
Owners: Mobile engineering; Content; Data & AI
Success signal: improved sleep (KR3). Opt-in rate shows whether the data advantage is real (KR1)

Next 3-6 Months: Remove the next churn drivers and deepen support: load time under 2s (#14), referral-based crisis mode (#9), Apple Watch integration (#11)
Owners: Platform engineering; Clinical/Product; Partnerships & Data
Success Signal: Churn keeps falling after the load-time fix (KR2); crisis mode clears clinical scoping; Watch is opt-in for enough users to justify richer data

Later 6-12 Months: Expansion bets, not commitments: onboarding redesign, therapist matching, Fable for Teams, Spanish/Portuguese, web version
Owners: Assign when a bet is triggered
Success Signal: Each bet moves forward only when its trigger hits

Module 3
Team charter
What we own

We own the experience of existing Fable users aged 25-30 in NY, LA and Chicago: helping them sleep well through difficult periods and find self-care beyond sleep. Growth owns getting new users in the door; we own what happens once they are here.

How we decide

The owner of an area decides inside it. Overlaps go to a named joint call, then up a fixed escalation path with a clock on every step, so a team-only conflict is settled within 5 business days and a cross-team one within 15.

Module 5
Financial model
The business case

Business Case: Personalized Daily Check-Ins
The ask
Fund opt-in personalized daily check-ins (Rock #6) for Q4 2026 launch, with a binding kill criterion at 8 weeks and end of Q1 2027.
The bet protects about $529K ARR by end of Q3 2027 and tests whether our historical-data differentiator is real.
Open question: build and staffing cost.
The return below needs to clear it.

The bet
• Outcome: Current users aged 25–30 who opt in get daily check-ins built on their own sleep history. They sleep better (KR1) and stay longer (KR2). • Who it serves: 336,000 users, the 8% of our 4.2M base in the target group. • Mechanism:
Personalized check-ins lead to better-reported sleep. Better sleep cuts quarterly churn among opted-in users from 10% to 7%. Each retained user protects about $70/year in revenue.

• Strategic test: The opt-in rate tells us whether users will let us use their history, which is the basis of how we win.

Expected return
The bet protects about $529K ARR by end of Q3 2027 and delivers 37.5% of the KR2 churn goal. • Selection bias: Users who opt in are likely our most engaged. A 10% holdout separates real lift from who signed up.

• Success churn: Users whose sleep improves may leave because they got better, so KR1 gains can raise KR2 churn. • Privacy trust: Check-ins run only on opted-in data, per our hard no. Any misuse would damage the differentiator itself.

Kill criterion
We stop if either of these is true: • Opt-in is below 15% of offered users within 8 weeks of launch. • Opted-in quarterly churn is not at least 1.5 pts below the 10% holdout by end of Q1 2027.
